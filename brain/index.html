Quantitative analysis

Every figure below is computed from the census graph: 118 nuclei, 1,090 directed tracts, weight = number of named references crossing the tract.

Fig. 1 · Adjacency

Connectivity matrix, 118 × 118

Row nucleus projects to column nucleus. Ordered by region, then volume. Cell opacity is log tract weight; colour is the target region.

Fig. 2 · Inter-regional flux

Tract counts between regions

Rows are source regions, columns are targets. Small figure is summed weight Σw. Diagonal is intra-regional.

Fig. 3 · Network roles

Afferent versus efferent load

Each nucleus by in-degree and out-degree, sized by volume. Dashed lines are population means.

Fig. 4 · Degree distribution

Heavy-tailed connectivity

Nuclei per degree bin (width 4). Amber is the mean, pink the median.

Fig. 5 · Volumetrics

Parenchymal volume by region and nucleus

Area is bytes. Fill strength is degree: brighter nuclei are more connected.

Signal arrivals per nucleus

One row per nucleus, last 10 seconds. Signals are an animation weighted by real tract strength, not recorded activity.
