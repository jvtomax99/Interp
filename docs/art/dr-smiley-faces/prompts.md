# Dr. Smiley: prompts for new faces

Use an image tool that edits a picture you upload (ChatGPT image editing,
Gemini, or similar). For every prompt, upload dr-smiley-reference.png
first. Make one face per request. Keep a result only if his head, size and
position match the reference exactly; otherwise ask again.

## The opening (paste this before each face)

Edit this image. Keep everything identical: the same character, head
position, head size, angle, framing, hair, ears, nose, cheeks, skin
tones, lighting, white coat, lanyard, eyebrows and the plain white
background. Do not move, zoom, crop, tilt or redraw the head. Change ONLY
the eyes (lids, irises, pupils) and the mouth, as described below. Same 3D
cartoon style, same render quality. Output the same size and aspect ratio
(4:5).

## 1. Surprised
Eyes wide open, upper lids raised so white shows all around the irises,
pupils a little smaller. Mouth a small, round open "O", lips relaxed,
a dark inside with a hint of teeth.

## 2. Sad
Upper eyelids heavy, covering the top third of the irises, eyes looking
slightly down, a soft wet shine. Mouth closed, corners turned down,
lower lip pushed up slightly.

## 3. Worried
Eyes open normally, looking slightly to his right, lower lids a little
tense. Mouth closed, tight and slightly wavy, one corner pulled down,
like a small uneasy grimace.

## 4. Doubtful
His left eye (on the right of the picture) squinted half closed, the other
eye open normally. Mouth closed, pulled to one side (toward the picture's
right), a small skeptical smirk.

## 5. Talking mouths (optional, four separate requests)
Eyes exactly as in the reference. Mouth only:
- "A": open wide and round, tongue visible, teeth on top.
- "E": open wide and flat, teeth showing, corners stretched.
- "O": rounded, lips pushed forward, small opening.
- "M": lips pressed closed, slightly pursed.
