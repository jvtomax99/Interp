# Kenney Car Kit (CC0)

https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit, licensed **Creative Commons CC0** (public domain; no credit required, given
anyway): "Car Kit" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl).

Downloaded 2026-11-03 from a copy of the kit on GitHub (`kvark/raycraft`, `data/car-kit/`), since kenney.nl serves its
zip through a download form. Unchanged here; `../../real-map/cars_bake.py` turns them into the campus's cars.
