# ChatGPT prompts for the Meshy figures

Attach `docs/art/figures-reference.png` with each. The nurse's prompt (it made
`docs/art/nurse-single.png`) is in `docs/handoff.md`, "Meshy nurse"; these two
follow it word for word except for the person.

## Doctor (left in the reference)

Using the attached picture as the character reference, draw ONLY the doctor on
the left (the young man with short dark brown hair, blue eyes, white lab coat
over teal scrubs, stethoscope around his neck, black shoes), alone, as a single
full-body character for 3D modeling. Keep him exactly the same: same face, same
hair, same lab coat with its pockets and name badge, same teal scrubs and teal
trousers with turned-up cuffs, same black shoes, same soft clay-toy 3D style and
colours. Make the stethoscope thick and chunky, lying flat against his chest and
shoulders, with no part hanging loose or away from the body. Keep the lab coat
closed enough that it does not flare out from his legs. Pose: standing straight,
facing the camera directly, arms hanging slightly away from his body so there is
a gap between arms and torso (and between arms and coat), hands relaxed, feet
slightly apart. Camera: straight on at chest height, no perspective tilt, whole
body in frame with a little space around him. Lighting: soft, even, flat front
lighting, no strong shadows, no rim light. Background: plain solid light grey,
nothing else in the picture. Square image, as large and sharp as possible.

## Visitor (right in the reference)

Using the attached picture as the character reference, draw ONLY the visitor on
the right (the young woman with big curly orange hair, green eyes, pale
skin, mustard-yellow ribbed turtleneck sweater, dark navy trousers with
turned-up cuffs, brown shoes), alone, as a single full-body character for 3D
modeling. Keep her exactly the same: same face, same hair shape and curls, same
sweater with its ribbed collar, cuffs and hem, same navy trousers, same brown
shoes, same soft clay-toy 3D style and colours. Keep the hair as big rounded
clay shapes that stay close to her head, with no strands floating free. Pose:
standing straight, facing the camera directly, arms hanging slightly away from
her body so there is a gap between arms and torso, hands relaxed, feet slightly
apart. Camera: straight on at chest height, no perspective tilt, whole body in
frame with a little space around her. Lighting: soft, even, flat front
lighting, no strong shadows, no rim light. Background: plain solid light grey,
nothing else in the picture. Square image, as large and sharp as possible.

Save them as `docs/art/doctor-single.png` and `docs/art/visitor-single.png`.

## Why the extra lines

Thin parts that stick out (a dangling stethoscope, a flared coat, loose curls)
are what 3D makers turn into blobs or holes, so each prompt asks for them to sit
close to the body. Everything else is the nurse's prompt unchanged, so the three
pictures come out in the same light and the same pose.
